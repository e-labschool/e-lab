## IB wording: verified vs reasoned
Verified by WebSearch/WebFetch against ibchem.com (IB25 pages R2.2.x, a third-party transcription of the 2025 guide, not the official PDF):
- R2.2.1 rate = change in concentration of a reactant/product per unit time (guidance: rates from tangents of graphs).
- R2.2.2 "Species react as a result of collisions of sufficient energy and proper orientation."
- R2.2.3 factors: pressure, concentration, surface area, temperature (and catalyst).
- R2.2.4 "Activation energy, Ea, is the minimum energy that colliding particles need for a successful collision leading to a reaction."
- R2.2.5 "Catalysts increase the rate of reaction by providing an alternative reaction pathway with lower Ea."
- Maxwell-Boltzmann guidance: "Construct Maxwell-Boltzmann energy distribution curves to explain the effect of temperature on the probability of successful collisions"; raising T flattens the curve and moves the peak right.
Reasoned, not verified against a source: the exact numbering of the Maxwell-Boltzmann statement, the in-sim wording of captions, the 40 % catalyst Ea reduction (a demo choice), and the Ea range 5-60 kJ/mol (a demo scale, stated in the UI; real Ea of 50-100 kJ/mol would give fractions of 10^-8 or less at room temperature and nothing would be watchable).

## Round 3: Collision Theory correction (spec docs/COLLISION_THEORY_CORRECTION_SPEC.md, 2026-11-20)
Scope: `src/engines/collision-theory-visualizer/` only (Chocolate Wrapping untouched; it shares no code with the files changed). Files: `lib/collisionEngine.js`, `lib/rateMetrics.js`, `lib/maxwellBoltzmann.js`, `lib/snapshot.js`, `lib/scenarios.js`, `components/RateGraph.jsx`, `components/MbGraph.jsx`, `components/PredictPanel.jsx`, `CollisionTheoryVisualizer.jsx`, `CollisionTheory.css`; tests `scripts/test-collision-model.mjs`, `scripts/test-collision-ui.mjs`, `scripts/verify-kinetics.mjs`.

### Model (spec 1, 5)
- The reaction is irreversible A + B -> C + D. The steady-supply / replenishment mechanism (the `steady` parameter, 1.5 s restore timers, site restore) is deleted, including in the solid system: a used-up solid site stays used up and its B becomes D for good.
- Particle units are conserved (`unitsTotal()`; in the solid system a used-up site counts as C). When the limiting reactant is used up no reaction can happen and the rate is 0.
- Success still needs relative collision energy >= Ea AND orientation; molecular kinetic energy (MB curve), relative collision energy, Ea, orientation, collision frequency and rate remain separate quantities (detail popover rewritten to say so).
- Design decision: the Concentration slider now adds or removes only the DIFFERENCE in reactant particles (it used to top reactants up to the slider value, which would be replenishment). Changing surface area mid-run keeps the number of used-up sites (outermost first).
- Predict-first reveal now measures the INITIAL rate of a fresh closed vessel (first 6 s, 6 seeds) because a long average no longer makes sense once reactants deplete; the wording says "initial rate".
- Behaviour to expect: at the defaults (T 400 K, 40 + 40) the rate starts near 1 to 5 reactions/s and has fallen to about a tenth within roughly 15 s, then has a long thin tail (second-order kinetics); a full run to about 95 % takes on the order of 100 s of simulated time.

### Rate graph (spec 2, 3)
- One smooth blue curve. Computed from the recorded time of every successful collision (`engine.successTimes()`): Gaussian-weighted local-linear slope of the cumulative reaction count, with a width that adapts so that about 12 reactions fall in the window (2 to 15 s). Nothing is hard-coded or forced to fall (tested: events that speed up give a rising curve, evenly spaced events a flat one, none a zero line).
- x = Time / s, y = Reaction rate / reactions s^-1; the x axis grows to cover the whole run. Products view: cumulative reactions (C formed) against time with the dashed tangent and a dotted "most possible" line; it plateaus when the limiting reactant is used up, and the graph says so.
- All histogram/bars code, state, imports and controls removed from both graphs (`binnedCounts`, `smoothedRate`, `energyHistogram`, `HIST_BIN`, `showHist`, "Show vessel energies" toggle, "Steady supply" toggle). A source-scan test and DOM tests (no `<rect>` outside clip paths) guard this. Dashed vertical LINES that mark "conditions changed" remain; they are not bars.
- Known imperfection: the right-hand end of a live curve is the least certain part (it only has past events); in the sparse late stage the curve can drift slightly up or down by a few hundredths of a reaction per second from random clustering.

### Maxwell-Boltzmann graph (spec 4)
- The density was already the correct 3-D kinetic-energy form f(E) = 2 sqrt(E/pi)(kT)^(-3/2) exp(-E/kT) (area 1, mode RT/2); the problem was the fixed 0-70 kJ/mol axis. Now `mbAxis()` sets x up to about 6.5 kT rounded up to a round number (15 to 40 kJ/mol between 250 and 700 K) and y to about 12 % above the peak; only the axis changes, never the curve. The curve still moves, flattens and shifts right with T because the limits change in coarse steps.
- When Ea is beyond the x range, the Ea line is flagged at the edge, a note explains, and a "Show full range" / "Zoom to peak" button (replacing the old y-axis "Zoom tail", which cut the peak off) extends x to Ea without changing the curve or the y scale.
- Catalyst: the curve is identical with the catalyst on (tested at DOM level); the effective-Ea line moves left (60 %), the single subtle shading follows it, the uncatalysed Ea stays as a dashed grey line. Added readout "Peak, RT/2" (1.66 kJ/mol at 400 K) and a tooltip/detail note that the shaded area is the fraction of molecules able to react, not the fraction of collisions that react.

## Round 3b: smooth Products curve, Reactants / Both views, species selection, rate-curve end artefacts (owner reference graphs)
Changed after the owner's two reference graphs (smooth cumulative curve to a plateau; reactant falling and product rising with markers, crossing). Everything below keeps the irreversible reaction, no bars, data from the engine and no hard-coded shape.

- **Selector** is now Rate | Reactants | Products | Both (`RateGraph.jsx`, `CollisionTheoryVisualizer.jsx`). Rate keeps its single blue curve.
- **Products**: cumulative C (and D, dashed) from the real events, counted at 36 evenly spaced times and joined by a monotone cubic (PCHIP) curve (`rateMetrics.productCurve`): never falls, never overshoots, passes through the real counts, levels off by itself; dashed tangent and dotted "most possible" line kept; plateau label when the limiting reactant is used up. Time axis starts at 10 s and grows to cover exactly the run so far (no fixed 500 s).
- **Reactants**: amount of A and of B left (solid system: the solid's unused sites A and B), from new engine history arrays (`hA`, `hB`, sampled every 0.5 s and cleared by Reset). In the mixed system A and B are always equal, so B is dashed over A.
- **Both**: one reactant (A; B in the solid system) falling in purple and one product (C; D in solid) rising in green, shape-preserving curves through counts sampled at 36 regular times with a circle / square marker at each, legend, "Number of particles" axis. The last sample is the live count, so the graph ends exactly on the engine's numbers. They cross and end at 0 and the total.
- **Reaction equation** `A + B → C + D` (`components/ReactionEquation.jsx`) sits above the graph; A, B, C, D, the arrow and "All" are native buttons with `aria-pressed`, a visible selected state and a focus ring. Click A or B: Reactants view narrowed to that species; click again: both reactants; C or D: Products narrowed; arrow or All: Both. It shares one state with the selector, and Reset returns to Rate with nothing selected.
- **Rate curve**: back to a local-linear slope of the cumulative count (a kernel density estimate was tried and rejected: its end correction over-estimated the thin tail), with a wider adaptive window (about 10 real events, 2 to 30 s). At the start there is no blurred hump and at the end no rise: over 14 seeds the start is at least 0.95 of the early peak and, once 80 % has reacted, the plotted rate never rises by more than 2 % of its peak (measured worst case about 0.4 %).
- **Default particle numbers unchanged**: the default 40 + 40 vessel gives 34 to 38 reactions in 150 s over the seeds checked (test asserts 30 to 45), so the curves are well resolved. The 8-reaction curve in earlier screenshots was the small-vessel (n = 8) exhaustion check, not the default.
- Tests: model 86 checks (new: PCHIP properties, default resolution, smooth Products curve, A/B/C history = engine counts, solid history, rate-curve end artefacts), UI 35 checks (new: selector order, equation states and synchronisation, per-species curves, Both markers and legend, solid-system views, Reset), changed expectations marked "[CHANGED]". `verify-kinetics.mjs` gained Reactants / Products / Both / species-selected screenshots and checks (`ct-*-g-reactants-full-run.png`, `-products-full-run`, `-both-full-run`, `-species-A`, `-species-C`).
- Still imperfect: the real data are stepped (reactions come in bursts, e.g. a plateau around 32 then a further step), so the smooth curves show gentle shoulders rather than a textbook exponential; the Reactants view cannot show A and B apart in the mixed system because they are equal; the Rate-curve window at the live edge is still the most uncertain part.
